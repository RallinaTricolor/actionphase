# Results Board

The results board is a GM's workspace for turning an action phase's
submissions into results. You read each submission and pull out the plans
that matter, lay them out by place and time, decide what happens where they
collide, write those events once as scenes, and then write each player's
result from a brief of everything that happened to them.

It's optional, and you choose each time. The Actions tab's results tools
still work, and you can switch between the two whenever you like: result
drafts, staged parts and sheet updates are shared by both.

## Opening the board

- **From the Actions tab:** during an action phase, **Open results board**
  sits above the results.
- **From History:** a past action phase whose board was used has **Open
  results board** beside its title. It opens read-only: see
  [When a phase ends](#when-a-phase-ends).
- **Results Board Setup**, in the game's **⋯** menu, opens
  [Game setup](./results-board-setup) without a board: useful before the
  first action phase starts.

The board needs a screen at least 1024 pixels wide. On a smaller screen it
says so; you can still write results from the Actions tab.

## The tabs

The board works through a phase in four steps, then has two tabs for your
reference material:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| [Read](./results-board-read) | Read each submission and make **cards** from the plans that matter. |
| [Map](./results-board-map) | Lay the cards out by place and time, give each an outcome, and find where people meet. |
| [Scenes](./results-board-scenes) | Write what happens where people meet, once, and choose who reads each part. |
| [Write](./results-board-write) | Write each player's result from their brief, check it, and publish. |
| [Codex](./results-board-codex-notes#codex) | The game's places, people, items and secrets. |
| [Notes](./results-board-codex-notes#notes) | Your own notes, for this phase and for every phase. |

Each step's tab shows how far along you are: "2/3 read", "4 to resolve",
"1/2 done", "0/3 ready". The tab you're on is part of the page's address, so
the browser's Back button and links work.

**Game setup**, at the top right, holds what applies to the whole game
rather than one phase: the codex, character names, reward lists and the
reward block. See [Game setup](./results-board-setup).

## Saving

Everything saves as you go. The header says **Saving…** and then **Saved**,
and offers to retry if saving fails. Co-GMs can work on the same board at
once; each sees the other's changes within a few seconds. If someone else
changes what you were editing, the board shows their version and says so,
with **Use mine** to put your change back.

Deleting something, or making a change to several things at once, shows a
message with **Undo**.

## Words the board uses

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Card** | One plan or action, usually quoted from a submission: "I lift the crown from the courier's satchel." |
| **IF card** | A plan that only happens if something else does: "If anyone follows me, I lose them on the bridge." It waits until it triggers. |
| **Time slot** | A column on the Map: a stretch of the phase, like "Dusk" or "Midnight". |
| **Outcome** | What became of a card: Happened, Partly, Failed, Interrupted or Moot. |
| **Scene** | Where people meet: a place, a time slot, a cast, and what happens there. |
| **Moment** | One thing that happens in a scene, with a list of who reads it. |
| **Stance** | How one character sees another this phase: Ally, Deal, Neutral, Wary, Target, or Ally ⇢ Target. |
| **Codex** | The game's reference list: locations, NPCs, items, factions, objectives and secrets. |
| **Brief** | Everything that happened to one character, in order, to write their result from. |

## The example phase

Each page of this guide follows the same small phase from start to finish.

**The game.** A city of guilds and thieves. This phase, *The Night Market*,
the Ember Crown is carried through the market by a courier on its way to the
palace.

**The players.**

- **Mara Quill**, a thief, means to steal the crown. She thinks Sable is
  her friend.
- **Jory Vance**, a sellsword, has been hired to guard the courier.
- **Sable Ott** has offered to help Mara get away, and plans to shoot her on
  Lantern Bridge and take the crown for herself.

**The GM's own pieces.** Captain Hale of the city watch, an NPC. A secret
only Jory knows: the crown is a forgery.

By the end of the phase, Mara will have lifted the crown, Jory will have
chased her onto the bridge, Sable will have sprung her ambush, and the watch
will have arrived. Each of them gets a result that tells only what they saw.

Start with [Game setup](./results-board-setup).

## When a phase ends

A phase isn't over until every result you're going to publish is published.
Once another phase starts, the one before it is read-only:

- Its board can be read but not changed, and that includes its list on Game
  setup.
- Its results stay as they are. Unpublished drafts stay unpublished, and can't
  be edited, published or deleted.
- Parts already published and waiting on their timer still go out.

To do more work on it, go to the **Phases** tab and **Activate** it again. That
makes it the current phase, so the phase you left becomes read-only instead,
and everyone gets the usual notice that a phase has started.

If a phase is about to end with drafts you still want to send, publish them
first. **Activate** offers **Publish & Activate** when there are any, but a
phase scheduled to start on its own doesn't ask.

## Who can see the board

The GM, co-GMs, and admins in admin mode can see and change it. Nobody else
can see it unless you turn on **Share the results board** in Game setup: then
the audience can watch every phase's board, read-only, and once the game is
archived anyone who can see the game can. See [Audience](./audience#the-gm-s-results-board).
