# Results Board: Read

Read is where a phase starts. It shows one submission at a time. You pull
the plans that matter out of it as **cards**, which the
[Map](./results-board-map) then lays out by place and time.

## Reading a submission

The toolbar lists everyone who submitted. A ✓ means you've finished reading
them, and the badge counts their cards. Pick a name to read that submission.

- Other characters' names and names from the
  [codex](./results-board-setup#codex) are highlighted, with locations and
  secrets in colours of their own. Your notes on each codex entry are under
  **Codex matches**, beside the text.
- **On the sheet:** above the text lists things from the character's sheet
  that the submission mentions. Click one to see its description.
- Text that's already on a card is highlighted. Click it to open the card.
  The number beside a paragraph counts the cards made from it.

When you're done with a submission, press **Done reading → next**. It marks
the submission read and opens the next one you haven't read. After the last,
the board offers **Go to the Map**. If you leave a submission with cards on
it for another without marking it read, the board says so, with **Mark read**
one click away.

## Making cards

Card only what matters: the plans that could meet someone else's, or that
you'll need to decide an outcome for. You don't need to card scenery.

- **Select a sentence or two** and choose **+ Card**, or **+ IF card** for a
  plan that only happens if something else does.
- **Hover a paragraph** for **+ Whole paragraph** and **+ as IF**.

The board places each new card for you:

- **Location:** one the carded text names. Otherwise, the location of this
  character's card from earlier in the submission, then any location the
  submission names, then the first. Every card needs a location, so add this
  phase's [locations](./results-board-map#locations) before you start.
- **Time slot:** the slot of the character's card from earlier in the
  submission. Otherwise the first slot, or the second if the first is
  "Pre-Scenario". A new board starts with four slots: Pre-Scenario, Slot 1,
  Slot 2, Slot 3. You can rename and add slots on the Map.
- **IF cards** wait in **Contingencies** until you decide they've triggered.
  The board fills in the condition from text that opens with "If", "When",
  "Unless" and the like.

A message says where the card went, with **Open card**. Placement is only a
first guess: moving cards is quick on the Map.

### The card panel

Opening a card shows it beside the submission, without hiding the text.
It's the same panel as on the Map:

- **From the submission:** the card's copy of the quote. Editing it doesn't
  change the submission.
- **Label on the board:** a short name for the Map, if the quote is long.
- **Split at the cursor:** turn one card into two. The text after the cursor
  becomes a new card in the next time slot, for plans like "plant the sword
  now, use it later".
- **Outcome**, its causes, a private **GM note**, **Location**, **Time
  slot**, and **IF card** with its **Condition**. These are covered on the
  [Map](./results-board-map#outcomes-and-causes) page.
- **Delete card**, with Undo.

## Other things to do with selected text

- **+ Codex** adds the selected name to the codex. A small dialog opens with
  the name filled in, so you don't lose your place.
- **+ Award** gives the character a reward for that passage, from your reward
  lists or as a new line, and keeps the passage as the reason. See
  [Rewards](./results-board-rewards#giving-rewards).

## Around this submission

With no card open, the panel beside the submission shows how this submission
connects to the others:

- **Stances:** what this character intends toward each other player. Pick
  one from the list: Ally, Deal, Neutral, Wary, Target, or Ally ⇢ Target (an
  ally for now, with a strike planned). Their stance back shows beside it.
- **Board cards:** this character's cards, with where and when each sits.
- **Names** and **Named by:** who this submission mentions, and whose
  submissions mention this character.
- **Same place, not named:** others whose submissions name the same places
  without naming this character, or being named by them. It's often where
  the surprise meetings are.
- **Codex matches:** codex entries in the submission, with your notes.

## The example

The three submissions for *The Night Market*:

> **Mara Quill:** At dusk I slip between the stalls of the night market and
> lift the Ember Crown from the courier's satchel.
>
> If anyone follows me, I lose them in the crowd.
>
> Then I cross Lantern Bridge to meet Sable at the Gilded Cup.

> **Jory Vance:** I walk beside Magister Vell's courier through the market,
> blade loose in its sheath.
>
> If anyone goes for the satchel, I draw and give chase.

> **Sable Ott:** Before dusk I tell Mara to meet me at the Gilded Cup, by
> way of the bridge. Then I wait under Lantern Bridge with my crossbow.
>
> When Mara crosses, I shoot her and take the crown.

Reading Mara, the GM selects the first paragraph's plan and presses
**+ Card**. The text names the market, so the card goes to **The Night
Market · Slot 1**. The second paragraph is a contingency, so the GM uses
**+ as IF**, and it waits in Contingencies with the condition "If anyone
follows me". The third paragraph names Lantern Bridge, so that card goes to
**Lantern Bridge**, in Slot 1 like her earlier card. It belongs later in the
evening; the GM will move it on the Map.

Mara counts Sable as an ally, so under **Stances** the GM sets Mara toward
Sable to **Ally**. Then **Done reading → next**.

Reading Jory, the GM selects "Magister Vell" and chooses **+ Codex** to add
him as an NPC. Jory's two paragraphs become a card at the market and an IF
card. **Same place, not named** lists Mara: they both mention the market,
though neither knows the other is coming.

Reading Sable, the GM sets Sable toward Mara to **Ally ⇢ Target**. Since Mara
thinks they're allies, the stance table on the Map will mark it as an
ambush. Sable's wait under the bridge becomes a card at Lantern Bridge, and
"When Mara crosses, I shoot her and take the crown" becomes an IF card with
the condition "When Mara crosses".

Every submission is read. Next: [the Map](./results-board-map).
